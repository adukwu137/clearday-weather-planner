Terms of use

ClearDay is a student project built for a coursework assignment. It is free to use and comes without any warranty.

Forecasts can be wrong

Weather and air quality data are model forecasts. Do not rely on ClearDay for decisions that affect safety or health. For official warnings, check your national weather service.

Advisories and ratings

Forecast advisories and outdoor ratings are calculated by ClearDay from forecast values using simple thresholds. They are not official warnings.
