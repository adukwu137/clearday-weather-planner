Privacy

ClearDay has no accounts and sets no cookies.

What stays in your browser

Your favorite cities, recent searches, last viewed city and unit choice are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device, and you can remove them with the Clear history button or by clearing site data in your browser.

What is sent to the server

When you search or open a city, your browser sends the city name or its coordinates to the ClearDay server. The server forwards them to Open-Meteo to get weather, air quality and city data. Open-Meteo receives the request from the server, not from your browser.

Logs

The ClearDay server logs the page requested, the response status and the response time. It does not log the city you searched for. The web server and load balancer in front of it may keep standard access logs that include IP addresses.

Third parties

Weather data comes from Open-Meteo.com. See its terms for how it handles requests.
